# Cyber Attack Impact Simulator

## Overview

The **Cyber Attack Impact Simulator** is an interactive tool designed to model and visualize the operational and financial impact of real-world cyber threats on enterprise environments. It enables users to simulate various attack scenarios and analyze their effects across critical business metrics.

---

## Objective

To provide a practical understanding of how cyberattacks impact organizations by simulating:

* System compromise
* Data exfiltration
* Operational disruption
* Financial loss

The project also highlights the importance of implementing modern cybersecurity controls and risk mitigation strategies.

---

## Key Features

* Interactive simulation of cyber attack scenarios
* Real-time visualization of impact metrics
* Support for multiple attack types
* Graph-based analysis of attack progression
* Estimation of financial and operational impact
* Integration of security best practices and mitigation strategies

---

## Simulated Attack Scenarios

* **Ransomware** – System encryption leading to operational downtime and financial loss
* **Supply Chain Compromise** – Exploitation through third-party dependencies
* **Malicious Insider** – Internal threat leveraging privileged access
* **Spear Phishing Attack** – Targeted social engineering leading to system compromise

---

## Metrics Tracked

* Systems Compromised
